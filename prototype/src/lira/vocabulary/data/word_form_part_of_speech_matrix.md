# Word Form to Part of Speech Matrix

Which word forms apply to which parts of speech, how each form is
actually recognised or derived, and why each form exists. `✓` means the
form applies to every word of that part of speech; `✓*` means it
applies only to a subset (e.g. not every pronoun has a distinct
reflexive case); `—` means the form does not apply to that part of
speech at all. Format lists the spelling rule(s) that produce the form;
String Pattern is the regex that recognises the identically-numbered
Format rule, or `N/A` where the rule can't be determined from spelling
alone and needs a lexical/curated source instead -- the same "declared
before it's populated" gap the POS subtype fields themselves currently
have (Noun.isCountable's own docstring, noun.ts).
